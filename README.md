# The Toe Nurse

This is the upgraded Nuxtjs/Tailwindcss 4x version of the site.

## Site Summary

The Toe Nurse is a mobile foot care service specializing in comprehensive foot health for individuals of all ages, 
emphasizing that foot care is essential for everyone, not just women. With over a decade of experience, the service is 
provided by a certified foot care specialist and registered nurse, offering personalized treatments directly at clients' 
homes or businesses. Services include nail trimming, callus and corn reduction, ingrown nail management, foot massages, 
and thorough foot assessments, all aimed at promoting healthy, pain-free feet. The Toe Nurse's mission is to encourage 
self-care and respect for foot health, ensuring that everyone can maintain strong and comfortable feet.
