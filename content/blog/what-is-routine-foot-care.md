Routine foot care typically refers to regular maintenance and hygiene practices aimed at keeping the feet healthy. It includes activities such as:

Regular Cleaning
: Washing the feet daily with soap and water to prevent bacterial and fungal infections.

Trimming Toenails
: Cutting toenails straight across to prevent ingrown nails and infections.

Moisturizing
: Applying moisturizer to prevent dry, cracked skin, especially on heels.

Checking for Issues
: Inspecting the feet for any signs of cuts, blisters, or sores that need attention.

Proper Footwear
: Wearing comfortable, well-fitted shoes that support the feet and allow them to breathe.

Managing Foot Odor
: Using foot powders or sprays to control odor and prevent fungal growth.

Foot Exercises
: Doing simple exercises to improve circulation and maintain flexibility.

Seeking Professional Care
: Visiting a podiatrist or foot specialist for any persistent foot problems, such as corns, calluses, or chronic pain.

Overall, routine foot care helps in maintaining foot health, preventing common foot problems, and ensuring comfort and mobility. 

Your feet are your number one means of transportation! Take care of them!!
