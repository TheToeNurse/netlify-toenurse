Hi there! I’m Trisha Vilen RN, BSN, PHN, CRRN, CFCS, After spending the first half of my life in the automotive industry where i concidered myself as a nurse for cars, which included years in parts, paint, a nationally certified tow truck driver, and a licensed locksmith, I took the leap to nursing following a personal battle with cancer and a need to be better informed about my health and welbeing. This is when i became a nurse for real and now have over 10 years experience and many letters behind my name. My journey has not been easy but has been about education, service and growth, and now I’m excited to introduce you to my new venture, "The Toe Nurse" serving the community one foot at a time.

## Why "The Toe Nurse"?

"The Toe Nurse" isn’t just a catchy name; from towing to toes its all about spelling and i have done both. I am commitment to bringing essential foot care to those who need it most: the hardworking folks who often neglect their feet and or hands. My experience with veterans and the automotive Industry has shown me the importance of proactive maintenance, especially among populations that may hesitate to seek medical attention or ask for directions.

## Services Offered

As "The Toe Nurse", I aim to revolutionize foot care for the blue-collar community. From routine foot maintenance to specialized care, my services include:

Routine Foot Care
: Nail trimming, callus management(both hands and feet), and skin moisturization.

Foot Health Education
: Teaching the importance of foot care and early detection. 

Mobile Foot Mechanic
: Bringing foot care services directly to your doorstep and redusing the need to be able reach and see your all toes.

Rounding Registered Nurse
: Providing holistic health checks to ensure overall well-being and providing referals when issues arise.

## Why Foot Care Matters

Just like cars need regular maintenance to run and drive smoothly, our feet require attention to prevent discomfort and even more serious issues. By staying ahead of foot problems, you can avoid unnecessary pain and maintain your mobility—essential for every aspect of daily life.

## Join Me on this Journey

My ultimate goal is to expand "The Toe Nurse" into a comprehensive mobile foot care service, reaching even more individuals who may not prioritize their foot, forget until they hurt or just cant reach their feet. Together, we can promote a positive approach to health and well-being within our community one visit at a time.

## Conclusion

"The Toe Nurse" isn’t just about treating feet; it’s about empowering individuals to take charge of their health. Whether you’re a veteran, a blue-collar worker, or anyone in between and or outside that box, I’m here to support you on your journey to healthier feet and a happier life.

Remember, your feet are your #1 means of transportation, From Towing to Toes, Maintenance Matters! Let’s change the stereo type and take the first step towards healthier feet together.
