### What Are Corns and Calluses?

Corns and calluses are thickened areas of skin that develop in response to friction and pressure. Corns typically form on the tops and sides of toes, while calluses often appear on the soles of the feet. Both can cause pain and discomfort if not managed properly

- **Professional Removal**: Safe and effective removal of corns and calluses using specialized tools and techniques.
