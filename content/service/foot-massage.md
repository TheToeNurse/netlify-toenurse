## Complementary Foot Massage

We offer a relaxing foot massage as a complementary part of our foot care services, whenever it is appropriate. This added benefit enhances your overall foot health and provides a soothing experience.

## Benefits of a Foot Massage

A foot massage enhances circulation, reduces swelling, and promotes relaxation, while also improving flexibility and easing stiffness in the feet and ankles. Overall, it supports better foot health and provides significant comfort and well-being.

**Improves Circulation**: Helps boost blood flow, reducing swelling and promoting healing.

**Reduces Stress and Tension**: Provides relaxation, easing muscle tension and stress in the feet.

**Enhances Mobility**: Aids in relieving stiffness and improving flexibility in the feet and ankles.

**Promotes Overall Foot Health**: Encourages better foot health by stimulating the feet and relaxing surrounding muscles.

## When Foot Massage May Be Skipped

While foot massages are beneficial, there are certain conditions where they may not be appropriate. For instance:

- **Athlete's Foot**: If you have athlete's foot or other fungal infections, foot massage may be skipped to avoid the risk of spreading the infection. Instead, we focus on effective treatment and maintaining foot hygiene.
- **Severe Foot Injuries**: If there are open wounds, severe injuries, or inflammation, massage may be deferred to prevent exacerbating the condition.
- **Other Health Conditions**: In cases of severe health conditions or contraindications, we may advise against foot massage to ensure your safety and well-being.

During your foot care appointment, we will assess whether a foot massage is suitable for your condition. Our priority is to provide the best care tailored to your needs and ensure that every aspect of your foot health is addressed.
